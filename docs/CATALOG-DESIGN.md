# Catalog design direction

Keep the supplied red/silver HCBTECH logo and the existing typography. Buyers need to choose coconut-processing equipment, inspect real photos, compare the listed configurations and contact the workshop. The memorable element is the actual workshop and machinery photography.

Palette: workshop charcoal #171717, steel gray #60676B, light steel #E8E4DC, paper #F2F0EB, logo red #C92525. Space Grotesk carries readable headings/body; IBM Plex Mono remains limited to existing technical labels. Avoid adding decorative numbering or animation to the new product content.

Layout: left-aligned introduction beside the real workshop photo, followed by three machine-family cards, a short needs guide, and purchasing questions. On mobile, keep the introduction compact and put the catalog action before the photo.

```
[ workshop introduction | real workshop photograph ]
[ grating family ][ shell peeling ][ milk pressing ]
[ needs guide: family → drive → request ]
[ questions / contact ]
```

Review against the brief: use one family per card, with its listed variants on the detail page. Do not duplicate a product for every camera angle. List prices are source references with reconfirmation wording. Photos remain family references until exact variant matches are confirmed. The two first GLBs are exterior-form pilots without measured scale, distinct from production-ready engineering models.
